Why an independent advisor

The BnB market is noisy. Good advice isn't.

Developers will sell you a unit. Operators will promise you occupancy. An independent advisor is the only party in the transaction who gets paid the same whether you buy or walk away.

No commissions, no conflicts

We're paid by you, not by developers or property managers. That means when a unit is a bad idea, we tell you.

Real data, not brochures

Actual occupancy, ADR, and net-yield numbers pulled from operating units — not the projected numbers from a developer's pitch deck.

Furnishing that pays back

We help you spend the right amount on furnishing — enough to photograph well and command premium rates, without over-capitalising.

Operator vetting

We've seen who actually delivers on cleaning SLAs, guest response times, and revenue reporting — and who quietly bleeds owners dry.

Exit planning from day one

Before you buy, we help you think through resale liquidity, building restrictions, and how the unit would price if you sold in 3–5 years.

You keep full ownership

The title stays in your name. The operator reports to you. We're your advisor — not a middleman taking a cut of your cash flow.
