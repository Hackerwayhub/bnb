FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before you reserve a unit.

You own a real, titled unit in a specific building. bnb.co.ke Invest furnishes it and operates it under a management agreement. Your name is on the title and you can sell or transfer the unit after the minimum holding period (typically 24 months).
The setup capital covers furniture, appliances, styling, photography, listing setup, and the first month of operating float. It does not include the purchase price of the unit — that is financed or purchased separately as part of your investment agreement.
Payouts are processed on the 5th of every month for the previous month's net rental income. You receive a full statement of bookings, revenue, expenses, and management fees alongside the payout.
Income fluctuates with occupancy. We mitigate low season through dynamic pricing, longer-stay discounts, and corporate contracts. Our estimates use conservative 12-month averages, not peak season figures.
Yes — you get 14 nights per year of owner use, subject to availability, booked through your owner dashboard.
Returns are not guaranteed. Occupancy, market conditions, and building quality all affect performance. We mitigate by only investing in vetted buildings in high-demand locations, maintaining a reserve fund, and reporting transparently every month.
