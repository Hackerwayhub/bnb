5 Cancellations & Refunds

Our standard cancellation policy is:

  • 7+ days before check-in: Full refund, minus any transaction fees
  • 3–7 days before check-in: 50% refund

Individual listings may have different cancellation policies — these are shown on the listing page before booking. If you need to cancel, contact us as early as possible.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method. Bank transfer refunds may take longer due to banking hours.
