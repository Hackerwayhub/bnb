On bnb.co.ke, the terms overlap but roughly mean:

  • BnB — a furnished apartment or house available for short stays, usually with a host nearby.
  • Staycation — a property designed for locals taking a break, often with premium amenities (pool, gym, views).
  • Holiday home — a standalone house or villa, often in coastal or safari locations, rented as a whole unit.

The labels help you filter, but the quality bar is the same across all three.
