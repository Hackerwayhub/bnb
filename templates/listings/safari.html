July–October is peak season for the Great Migration in the Maasai Mara. January–March is excellent for calving season and fewer crowds. Amboseli is best June–October for clear Kilimanjaro views. We'll advise based on your priorities.

Many lodges welcome children, though some have minimum age requirements (usually 6+ for game drives). We'll match you with family-friendly camps and can arrange private vehicles, shorter drives, and child-focused activities.
